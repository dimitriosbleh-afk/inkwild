# Fresh eyes on the reading islands, after Wave 3

Sample: Skills 1.2, 1.7, 2.1, 2.8, 3.3, 3.10, 4.2 and 4.8. For each I read the I do and its card, both We do tasks, the write task, every Band 1 and Band 4 Encounter, and the first two Band 3 Encounters. I also read Bands 1 and 4 of prologue.json and convince-heist.json.

**Overall: nearly at the bar.** The Tap to learn lessons are now real teaching. Every lesson works a concrete example, checks before moving on, and names a misconception children really hold. Band 4 now asks children to judge and explain, most of the time. 3.10 and 1.7 are the best things in the sample. What still loses a child is now narrower: one Skill (3.3) with real errors, a few shortcuts that remain, a write task with nothing after it, and a long road to the first Creature.

Verdicts: **meets**, **nearly**, **not yet**.

## Story chapters

The prologue and convince-heist at Band 1 meet: short sentences, the jokes survive, and Wren's "Found it" beat is kept. The Bilbolster narrating in first person quietly reuses 2.1. At Band 4, both are rich and reward inference, such as the purple smudge in the porthole and "a lie with no poles can still move a crowd". At 700 to 800 words they are long, but they are a reward, so that is fine for strong readers. One Band 1 gap: "a man in a striped suit" arrives in the heist with no word on who he is.

## 1. The three changes that would now make the biggest difference

**1. Get to the first Creature sooner, and write after playing, not before.** A first visit to a Skill is now 5 to 7 lesson steps, then 2 We do tasks, then a write task the child can't skip (activity.ts sends them to it before any Encounter). For a child who chose this over a book, that's a lot before the fun starts.
- Move the write task to after the Skill's third Encounter, or to the moment its Creature is caught, so the child writes once they've used the move.
- Then close the loop after DONE. At the moment, "HERE'S ONE WAY" is a dead end, and the ticks change nothing.
  - Show the model with its parts tagged in the lesson's colours, as on the Move card.
  - Ask one question: "Tap the part of YOUR sentence that does check 2." If they can't find it, offer an EDIT button.
- That turns self-ticking into checking against the test. That is the habit the island 8 and 9 work depends on.

**2. Give the Daily review consequences.** review.ts says "A review never changes a Band or mastery". So a Skill the child has forgotten stays mastered, and nobody, child or James, ever knows.
- On a first-try miss, open the Move card on the answer screen, and bring that Skill back in the next day's review.
- After two misses in a row, mark it "rusty" on its island and replay the lesson's trap step before the next Encounter.
- Every few days, make one of the three review items a write task from a mastered Skill, with a fresh `given`.
- This is retrieval practice plus feedback that says what to do next, both core to VTLM 2.0.

**3. Fix 3.3, then add rules for the last shortcuts.** 3.3 needs the pile renamed to "numbers or records". It also needs b3-1, b4-2 and b1-4 corrected, and its items rewritten so each kind appears in varied wording: an expert without "for N years", an example without a time opener, a record told without the word "records". After that, add three rules to rules.ts, because children learn these shortcuts faster than any reviewer spots them:
- a tapText answer may not be the longest part in more than half of a Skill's tapText tasks (1.2 is 4 of 4);
- in "which line would the writer use" tasks, no option may copy a passage sentence (1.2 b4-6);
- in a fix task, the right replacement may not be the antonym of the planted word (4.2 b4-3).

## 2. What a 10 to 12 year old would find boring or confusing

- **3.3 I do, step 3:** "All three kinds are evidence. But our piles use 'evidence' for numbers or records only." A weak reader will lose the thread here.
- **3.3-b3-1:** the child searches Dev's letter for the four sentences, which aren't in it.
- **3.3-b4-2:** they read 220 words of opinion piece, then sort seven items that aren't in it, after the passage promised six.
- **The write screen:** the ticks do nothing and DONE needs only four words, so some children will type four junk words to get to the Creatures. After DONE, "HERE'S ONE WAY" has nothing to do next.
- **4.8-b4-3:** nine lines, four piles of fancy mood words. It's long, fiddly tapping for no harder thinking.
- **2.8-b4-4:** the old painter announces the theme, so the child just taps the speech.
- **Repetition:** a kite stuck in a gum tree three times on islands 1 and 2. The custard gag (2.8 We do, 3.10 lesson, We do and write, prologue) is a running joke, which children like, but the kite is just reuse.
- **4.8-b1-2:** a child who remembers the lesson's kettle, soup and wolf answers without reading. That's not boring, but it is a free point toward mastery.

## 3. Is anything now worse than before?

- **Longer before play.** Each lesson is now 5 to 7 steps, each with a check, then 2 We do tasks, then a write task the child can't skip. That costs the most engagement at exactly the moment a child is deciding whether to keep playing. Change 1 fixes it without losing any of the teaching.
- **Band 4 reads heavier.** Around 220 words of passage, a 30-word prompt, then four "X, because Y" options (1.7-b4-1, 1.7-b4-3, 3.10-b4-5). For a strong thinker who is an EAL learner, the options are now the hard part. Keep the reasons, but cut each option to the claim plus one short reason.
- **Some Band 4 tasks only look harder.** A few are harder words wrapped around Band 1 or 2 thinking (4.8-b4-3, 2.8-b4-4, 2.8-b4-6). They aren't worse than before, but they make the ladder look climbed when it isn't.
- **Not worse:** Band 1 is still reachable in every Skill I read, and no lesson made a rule wrong. Wave 2 fixed that problem.
