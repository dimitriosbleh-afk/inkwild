## 1. 8.6-b4-3 (Band 4)



**Tap the sentence that doesn't belong, then choose one that fits.**

Text: "Indi's newest mural was painted entirely with recycled paint. Every tin came from the island tip, where people throw out half-used paint after fixing up their homes. She sorted the tins by colour on her back verandah, mixing leftovers to make new shades. Because the supply was so random, the sky ended up a strange purple-grey. Not a single one of her forty-two tins was bought from a shop. Next year, Indi hopes to enter a brand-new painting in a big art prize."

Wrong spot: **Next year, Indi hopes to enter a brand-new painting in a big art prize.**, fix: **When one colour ran out, she had to wait for someone to throw out more.** (other fixes: The mural shows the island ferry, the lighthouse and three pelicans., Indi has been drawing every single day since she could hold a crayon., Visitors can see the mural on the back wall of the Redpen Times office.)

Why: The paragraph is about the recycled paint. Waiting for someone to throw out more paint is part of that story.

> Wren saw a red-bellied black snake on the basalt ridge. She wrote lines for three readers: a ranger's field report, a sign for hikers on the path and a picture book for preschoolers.

- SNAKE AREA. If you meet a snake, stop, stay still and let it move away. → **sign for hikers**
- Sammy the snake slid under a warm, flat rock for a long afternoon nap, and nobody woke him up. → **picture book for preschoolers**
- One adult, about 1.2 metres long, was sighted at 9.20 am on the eastern scree slope. → **ranger's field report**

Why: Rangers need exact records, hikers need quick instructions and preschoolers need a gentle story.

> Gus put a snail on the table. And then the snail slid across the table and left a trail of slime across the table.
