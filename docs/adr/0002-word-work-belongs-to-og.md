# 2. Word work belongs to OG, not the game

Date: 2026-11-04
Status: accepted

## Context

The Victorian Curriculum Level 5-6 Phonic and word knowledge content (VC2E5LY03-05, VC2E6LY03-04) covers spelling, phonics and morphology. The school already teaches these daily through Orton-Gillingham (Yoshimoto), with fixed morpheme cards, keywords and routines.

## Decision

The game leaves out spelling, phonics, morphology/morphemes and handwriting entirely. It owns sentences and texts: comprehension, vocabulary from context, grammar for writing, text structure, author's craft and editing. Speaking and listening content is also out, as a solo iPad game cannot do it well.

## Consequences

- No risk of the game teaching a morpheme meaning or keyword that conflicts with the OG cards.
- A future reader seeing curriculum codes with no matching Branch should look here: the gap is intentional.
