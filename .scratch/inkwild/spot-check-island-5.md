## 3. 5.3-b3-5 (Band 3)



**Tap the pieces in order to make a sentence that makes sense.**

Pieces: The match went ahead | as planned | although | because | the forecast had promised storms.

Answer: **The match went ahead as planned although the forecast had promised storms.**

Why: Although. Promised storms should have stopped the match, so going ahead is a surprise.

## 4. 5.3-b1-2 (Band 1)

> Jai kept practising his kicks ___ he could hit the post.

**Which joining word fits?**

Options: **until** (answer) / because / although

Why: Until is about time. He practised up to the point where he could hit the post.

## 5. 5.3-b4-6 (Band 4)



**Match each half-sentence to the right shell.**

- whereas the north of the state is dry → **contrast**
- provided that the form is signed → **condition**
- given that the road was closed → **cause**
- even though nobody clapped → **contrast**
- as long as you're careful → **condition**

Why: Contrast sets two things against each other, a condition must be met first, and a cause explains why.
