# MAXX OUT: story bible

The year-long story in Chapters. Read this before writing any Chapter. Content rules (Australian spelling, no real brands or people, no First Nations stories) are in `docs/agents/content-writing.md`; length and sentence targets per Band are `CHAPTER_WORDS` and `BAND_TARGETS` in `src/content/rules.ts`.

**Premise.** Maxx Vantablack, the world's biggest streamer, is secretly capturing every rare Creature in the archipelago to star in his channel. His 40 million followers, the Vantafam, think he is rescuing them. The crew sails the leaky *Soggy Biscuit* from Island to Island, pulls a Heist on each of Maxx's bases, frees the Creatures and collects evidence that he is lying. In the finale they hijack his live stream to prove it.

**Maxx's catchphrases** (use one or two per Chapter he appears in): "Maxx it out, Vantafam!" / "Smash that heart button like it owes you money!" / "That's Maxx-imum content!" / "Two Xs, because one X is for losers." / sign-off "Stay gold!" His followers are the **Vantafam** (some older Encounters say "Maxxers"; Chapters use Vantafam).

**Maxx's stuff:** the gold jet ski *Gold Rush*; the floating mega-yacht *Vantadome*, with a glass dome and a waterslide off the back; MaxxCam drones that film everything; guards in gold tracksuits called **Goldies**.

**Keepers written so far:** **Captain Clause** (he), a huge old sea captain with a belt-length white beard: "I am not THAT Claus. Mine has an E on the end." He refuses to answer fragments ("That is not a sentence. That is a crumb. Who did what?"). Clawsette, a small blue crab, rides on his shoulder snipping. **Inspector Fennick** (she), long coat, frying-pan-sized magnifying glass, reads everyone at a glance, and never says anything directly: "I couldn't possibly say... but notice..." Owlibi, her owl, was always somewhere else the whole time. **Madame Pause** (she), tall, silver-haired ski instructor with a scarf so long it trails like a tail; she says her punctuation out loud ("Welcome, comma, Captain, full stop.") and is horrified by "Let's eat Madame Pause!" Stoppit, a round robin with a red chest like a stop sign, rides on her ski pole and pecks anyone whose sentence won't end. **Narra the Teller** (he), tall and barefoot, in a cloak stitched from old sails, each patch printed with a story; he talks about himself in the third person like a narrator ("Narra the Teller does not trust them. Not yet."), asks "Whose story is it?" and retells any event from the other side. Echobat, a tiny bat hanging from his straw hat, repeats the last word of everything ("Story," squeaks Echobat. "Story.") and can copy any voice it has heard often, including Maxx's. **Slick Vinnie** (he), market spruiker at the bottom of Bluster Rock: red-and-white striped suit, slicked-back hair, moustache waxed into two curls, a big red umbrella for a stall. He also runs a ghost tour of the old mine at the back of the rock. Everything he says is a sales pitch built from persuasive tricks ("Roll up, roll up!", "Ten thousand sold this morning!", "Everybody's wearing one!", "Can you really afford NOT to?"), and he ends with "Would I lie to you?" He respects only customers who don't buy. Spruikoo, his kookaburra, cackles "Koo-koo-ha-ha-HA!" whenever someone makes a claim they can't back up, which is terrible for business. **Poet Pip** (she), small, round misted-up glasses, a yellow raincoat covered in handwritten poems, a pencil behind each ear; paddles a tin bathtub with a broom. She never says anything plainly: every line is a simile or metaphor ("Your boat is a soggy sandwich, and you are the filling."), followed by "Picture it." When she does speak plainly, it means something is badly wrong. Similoon, a fluffy grey long-necked bird that nests on her head, turns into whatever she is compared to ("as round as a ball", "as fierce as a crocodile"), so the crew must be careful with similes near her. Each later Keeper needs one verbal habit tied to their Branch's Skill.

## Islands, bases and evidence

Each Island has an **arrive** Chapter (meet the Island, its Creatures and its Keeper, and spot the base) and a **Heist** Chapter (break in, free the Creatures, grab the evidence). Both must make sense in any order, so Island episodes never mention the twists as having happened. BOLT-9 and Kaydence don't appear in person; Wren *seeds* are fine (see below). The Keeper starts as a wary rival and ends as an ally once you've won the badge.

## Main story

- **Prologue** (`start`), "Maxx It Out!": the crew spots Maxx's own logo on a "rescue" net. Wren has a map of eight bases she "found". You decide to break them all out. Hook: Maxx holds up a photo of the *Soggy Biscuit*: "See you soon, Captain."
- **Old Glimmer.** A whale-sized glowing ray nobody has seen for fifty years. The Wishlist (Quillstone) names her as "THE BIG ONE" for Maxx's live finale. In the Echo Studio (Twotide) the last tape is her deep, slow song, labelled "THE BIG ONE. Play this to lure her close. Bring the biggest net." Any Heist may hint that Maxx is hunting something big.
- **3 badges, the Wren twist** ("The Family Special"): Maxx's tearful "Family Special" stream begs his runaway little sister "Wrennie" to come home, with a baby photo. It's Wren. Their real surname is Bottomley. They once nursed a possum together; then the channel ate him. She found the first cage room on the *Vantadome* (it is on **Deck 5**; the Sorry Script's scribbled-out "Version 27: Say sorry for what's on Deck 5 of the Vantadome. ONLY IF THEY FIND IT." seeds it), took his map and ran. You decide she stays. Hook: Kaydence Kray knocks on the hull and offers to help.
- **Between 3 and 7 badges**, Kaydence travels with the crew, "helping", which mostly means filming everything for her channel, her *Clickbait* tied alongside.
- **5 badges, BOLT-9 switches sides** ("Very Good, Sir. No.", *written*): Maxx sends BOLT-9 to fetch Wren home and orders him to delete his memories of her childhood (he was her nanny-bot). BOLT-9 projects one memory onto the sail first: little Wren and little Max feeding a baby possum, **Sir Nibbles**, in a shoebox, and Max promising to "save every animal in the world". He refuses ("Very good, sir. No."), dusts Maxx off the screen and joins the crew. He has recorded everything Maxx has ever done. Kaydence asks him a little too eagerly where Maxx keeps things; he rates only her ring light. Hook: BOLT-9 tells you the photo of the *Soggy Biscuit* that Maxx showed in the prologue was sent to Maxx by someone, on a call BOLT-9 was sent out of the room for, while Kaydence's camera blinks behind you.
- **7 badges, Kaydence double-crosses** ("Stay Cray!", *written*): the crew's evidence box is an old biscuit tin labelled **"NOT BISCUITS"**, kept in the fridge behind Mo's leftovers because nobody goes near Mo's leftovers. Kaydence films where it lives, steals it overnight and leaves on the *Clickbait* ("Thanks for the snacks! Stay cray! xx K."). On Maxx's stream from the *Vantadome* he shreds it, and she gets the co-host spot on his finale. She gloats that she sent him the photo of the *Soggy Biscuit* back in the prologue. BOLT-9's memory is the backup: he dusted every item in the tin and scans everything he dusts ("She did not take the butler. I rate her plan four out of ten."). Hook: a gold net the size of a footy field is craned onto the *Vantadome*, something huge glows beneath it, and BOLT-9 still has a key to the yacht. These chapters never list which pieces of evidence were in the tin, because the Islands done by then differ.
- **Finale**: "Biggest Rescue EVER, Live!" from the *Vantadome*, 40 million watching, Kaydence co-hosting, Old Glimmer in a gold net. The crew sneaks aboard; BOLT-9 plugs into the stream and plays all eight pieces of evidence; Wren steps into the shot: "Hi, Max. It's me." Old Glimmer and the Creatures go free, the Vantafam turns, Kaydence's own camera catches her gloating, and Maxx falls off the *Gold Rush*. Ending: Maxx, embarrassed but not a monster, starts doing real rescue work as a volunteer, and Wren gives him one small chance.

**Wren seeds** (one per Island episode at most): she knows things she shouldn't (where the vents are, his password habits, that he ticks lists in gold pen, that he sings flat, that he hides important papers in a box marked boring, that he's scared stiff of frogs), flinches at his catchphrases, then covers with "Lucky guess." They must read fine both before and after the reveal.

## Running jokes

- The *Soggy Biscuit* leaks whenever someone sneezes.

## Every Chapter

The same events at all four Bands, only the words change. Band 1 is about 250 words of short, plain sentences; Band 4 is about 700 with richer words and longer sentences. Separate paragraphs with a blank line. Find the funny: something gross, something Maxx says, something Mo says. End on a hook: a reveal, a threat or a question that makes the reader tap the next Chapter.
