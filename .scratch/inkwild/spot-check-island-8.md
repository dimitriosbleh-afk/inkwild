## 2. 8.2-b4-1 (Band 4)

> Draft: By lunchtime it had sorted every book by colour the librarian nearly fainted.

Zed's fix: By lunchtime it had sorted every book by colour. The librarian nearly fainted.

Indi's fix: By lunchtime it had sorted every book by colour, and the librarian nearly fainted.

**Who fixed the run-on correctly?**

Options: Zed, because Indi's comma still leaves two whole thoughts jammed into one sentence. / Indi, because a full stop would turn 'The librarian nearly fainted' into a fragment. / **Both, because each one puts the right thing between the two whole thoughts.** (answer) / Neither, because the joining word should come before the comma, not after it.

Why: Both. A full stop and a comma plus and each hold two whole thoughts properly. The writer can choose.
