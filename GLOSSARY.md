# Literacy Outcomes

A self-paced iPad game for a Grade 5/6 composite class that builds reading and writing at the sentence and text level, aligned to the Victorian Curriculum v2.0 English, played in students' own time.

## Language

### Curriculum shape

**Branch**:
A skill area of the game, hanging off the general theme, that maps to a cluster of Victorian Curriculum content descriptions.
_Avoid_: level, unit, module, world

**Skill**:
One teachable move inside a Branch, traced to one or more content description codes.
_Avoid_: lesson, objective, topic

**Band**:
One of four difficulty settings for a task, pitched at Level 4, 5, 6 or 7, tracked separately per Student per Branch.
_Avoid_: level, grade, tier

**Tide quest**:
The daily nudge that points a Student towards a Branch they have neglected, with a bonus reward.
_Avoid_: homework, assignment, daily task

**Word work**:
Spelling, phonics, morphology and handwriting: owned by the school's OG program and deliberately outside this game.
_Avoid_: vocabulary (vocabulary from context is in scope; word work is not)

### Learning moves

**Teach sequence**:
The in-game I do / We do / You do path every Skill passes through before it is ever tested.
_Avoid_: tutorial, onboarding

**Second look**:
The moment after a wrong move: a one-line reason, then another try, with nothing lost.
_Avoid_: penalty, fail, wrong answer screen

### The world

**Island**:
The place in the archipelago where one Branch lives; every Branch has exactly one Island.
_Avoid_: level, zone, map

**Creature**:
An original collectable companion whose type is a Branch; it is found and befriended by doing that Branch's Skills, and it evolves when a Skill is mastered.
_Avoid_: pet, monster, Pokémon

**Encounter**:
One task, framed as something happening in the world (a Creature tangled in a sentence, a claim to block), finishable in 2-4 minutes.
_Avoid_: question, quiz, exercise

**Chapter**:
One instalment of the year-long story, written at every Band, unlocked by progress.
_Avoid_: level, lesson, reading passage

**Maxx**:
Maxx Vantablack, the year-long story's villain: a mega-streamer secretly capturing rare Creatures for his channel while his followers believe he is rescuing them.
_Avoid_: the boss, the bad guy

**Heist**:
The Chapter-ending mission on each Island where the crew breaks Creatures out of one of Maxx's bases.
_Avoid_: raid, mission, level

**Keeper**:
The rival a Student battles to earn an Island's badge; the Keeper battle is that Branch's checkpoint.
_Avoid_: boss, gym leader, test

**Variant**:
A rarer look of a Creature the Student already has: shiny (a lucky find), golden (a run of first tries in its Skill) or Champion (Goldie battles won with it).
_Avoid_: skin, edition, tier

**Goldie battle**:
The daily review on Home Island: a Student picks one of their Creatures and fights one of Maxx's Goldies over three rounds drawn from Skills they have done.
_Avoid_: daily quiz, revision test

**Field Guide**:
The record of every Creature a Student has met or befriended, and of the Variants they hold.
_Avoid_: dex, collection, inventory

**Swap**:
Two Students sharing a Creature face to face via QR code; both keep a copy.
_Avoid_: trade

### Players

**Student**:
A Grade 5/6 player, anywhere from about 18 months below the start of Level 5 to 18 months above the end of Level 6.
_Avoid_: user, learner, kid

**Save**:
A Student's progress, held only on their own iPad; there are no profiles because every Student has their own device.
_Avoid_: account, login, profile

**Piece**:
A Student's own free writing from the Story workshop: never marked, kept only on their iPad, and seen by a teacher only when the Student chooses to show it.
_Avoid_: submission, entry, assignment

**Backup code**:
A 12-character code holding a Student's progress, copied into their book and typed back to prove it was copied correctly, used to restore the Save on any iPad; swapped Creatures and Variants are not in it.
_Avoid_: password, login code
