# Spot check: Comma Cay (Punctuation for meaning)

These are the 10 Encounters the automatic checks were least sure about. For each, tick **OK**, or write what is wrong. Two right answers, a confusing prompt, or a wrong answer are all worth a note.

## 1. 6.5-b4-3 (Band 4)



**Tap the pieces in order to make the sentence.**

Pieces: The whole crew | cheered for | Dr Ana Frost | , | the ship's captain | . | ,

Answer: **The whole crew cheered for Dr Ana Frost, the ship's captain.** or **The whole crew cheered for the ship's captain, Dr Ana Frost.**

Why: The extra part comes last, so one comma opens it and the full stop closes it.
