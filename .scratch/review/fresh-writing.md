# Fresh review: writing islands sample

Sample: 5.1, 5.4, 5.11, 6.5, 6.10, 7.5, 8.2, 8.9, 9.1, 9.5. For each I read the I do, both We dos, the write task (workshop for 9.x), all of Bands 1 and 4, and b3-1 and b3-3.

**Overall.** The teaching now meets the bar. Every I do works an example, names a real trap, and checks understanding before practice. 8.2, 6.5 and 8.9 are as good as a strong teacher's lesson. What would still lose a child:
- how long it takes to reach the first Creature, and how much reading that takes;
- a few shortcuts that still let a student pass without the Skill;
- the same idea getting different names on different islands.

Verdicts: **meets**, **nearly**, **not yet**. Q1 to Q6 are the six questions in the brief (I do, trap, Band 1, Band 4, write task, errors).

On 5.11's terminology:
- The curriculum code VC2E6LA06 does say "adverb groups".
- But the tasks also give that name to:
  - whole clauses: "whenever the alarm is switched on", "until its drill snapped";
  - noun groups: "every Tuesday night";
  - "to" clauses: "to get to the other side".
- 5.1 taught "When the rocket landed" as a dependent clause, so the same kind of chunk gets two names on one island.
- Writer brief rule 11 says not to classify "to" clauses.
- Step 4's line "This Skill calls them adverb groups" shows the child the doubt.

I'm not sure how the VC v2.0 glossary bounds the term. Either use "adverbials" as the umbrella term, bridged once, or keep the classified chunks to phrases and adverb-headed groups.

## The three changes that would make the biggest difference

**1. Let the crew talk.** Read the whole line aloud for an I do's `say`, `ask` and `win` lines, for nudges, hints and Second-look reasons, and for write-task asks.
- The I dos are now 270 to 400 words each (8.2: 401, 9.5: 386, 5.1: 365). Before Wave 2 they were about 60.
- `src/ui/speech.ts` speaks only a single tapped word.
- So a student 18 months below level, or a new EAL learner, has several minutes of decoding before the first Creature, on the screen that teaches.
- On-device en-AU speech is already used, so nothing leaves the iPad.
- Keep reading passages silent, since reading them is the Skill.

**2. Close the last shortcuts to mastery.**
- **Build tiles:** add the capital and full stop automatically, as Wave 2 planned. `joinTiles` in `src/core/check.ts` only tidies spaces, so the capitals and full stops on the tiles still give the order away (5.4 We do 2, 5.4-b1-5, 5.1-b4-6).
- **7.5:** in half the pattern tasks, have the prompt name the reader, not the pattern. For example, "Stuck players scan this walkthrough. Which opener helps them?" instead of "Each sentence starts by naming the room".
- **Small fixes:** change 6.5-b1-5 so that comma count doesn't decide the pile, and rewrite 5.4 We do 1's hint.

**3. One grammar across the islands, held in the Move cards.**
- Give each term one meaning everywhere. Today:
  - "joining word" means "and or but" in 8.2 but "when or although" in 5.4;
  - "whole thought" is never linked to "main clause";
  - the same clause is a "dependent clause" in 5.1 and an "adverb group" in 5.11.
- Put each card term's `means` in one shared list. Add a check that no tag has two different definitions on two islands.
- Settle 5.11's umbrella term (James's call).

## What a 10 to 12 year old would find boring or confusing

- **The first visit to any Skill.**
  - Order: the I do (6 or 7 steps), two We dos, then a compulsory "Your turn to write", before any Creature.
  - `activityScreen` sends the student back to the write card until they have written 4 words of their own.
  - A reluctant writer will type 4 junk words to get past it.
- **7.5 Band 4 tapText and fix** (b4-2's starship log, b4-3's possum report): about 200 words to read for a one-glance answer.
- **8.9's write task**, straight after b1-3: the same coach and the same score.
- **5.11 step 4**, "This Skill calls them adverb groups", from a student who learnt "dependent clause" on the same island.
- **Going from 6.10 to 9.1:** double speech marks are taught, then single ones are modelled.
- **The Story workshop screen:** after "I'M FINISHED" nothing invites a read-back or a second pass. It is just "saved".

## Is anything now worse than before?

- **Length.** The I dos are 5 to 6 times longer than before Wave 2, against the brief's target of about two minutes. The teaching is much better, but the reading cost falls hardest on the weakest readers the change was meant to help. Change 1 fixes most of it. Then trim one step per Skill where a step only restates (5.1 step 5, 9.1 step 2).
- **Write-task timing.** It comes before any practice and is never revisited, so it lengthens the first session without building retrieval. Move it to after the first three right-first-try Encounters. Bring one write card a week into the Daily review.
- **7.5 Band 4** got harder through length, not thinking. That is the opposite of what Wave 3 aimed for.
- **Nothing else got worse.** In this sample, the Second looks now ask a question instead of naming the answer, and the traps are real.
