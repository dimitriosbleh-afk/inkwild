**Match each sentence to the precise word that could replace 'loud'.**

- The kettle's whistle was loud: a thin, high scream. → **shrill**
- The applause was loud, like a storm rolling in. → **thunderous**
- The drums were loud and deep, and they shook the floor. → **thunderous**
- The TV in the lounge was loud, and nobody turned it down for hours. → **blaring**

Why: One vague word can need different precise words. The clues around it decide which.

## 3. 5.2-b4-6 (Band 4)

> Whenever the fog rolled in, the lighthouse keeper, who never believed in ghosts, locked every door twice.

## 7. 5.3-b3-5 (Band 3)

> The forecast had promised storms. The match went ahead as planned.

**Tap the pieces in order to join these into one sentence that makes sense.**

Pieces: Because | the forecast had promised storms, | The match went ahead as planned | although | the match went ahead as planned. | Although | until | the forecast had promised storms.

Answer: **Although the forecast had promised storms, the match went ahead as planned.** or **The match went ahead as planned although the forecast had promised storms.**

Why: Although. Promised storms should have stopped the match, so going ahead is a surprise.
