## 1. 3.1-b4-6 (Band 4)

> Reporter A: 'At 11:42 pm on Tuesday, a security camera filmed the brightest lights it has ever recorded above the old mine.' Reporter B: 'At 11:42 pm on Tuesday, a security camera filmed eerie green lights above the old mine.'

**The editor wants a news story with no opinions in it. Which is true about the two lines?**

Options: **Only A sticks to fact claims. B slips in the reporter's opinion.** (answer) / Only B sticks to fact claims. 'Brightest' is Reporter A's opinion. / Both stick to fact claims, because both give the time and the place. / Both slip in an opinion: 'brightest' in A and 'eerie' in B.

Why: The camera's footage could check 'brightest'. 'Eerie' is how Reporter B feels, so B has an opinion part.

**What kind of backing is the second pitch, and how does it compare with Rufus's story?**

Options: **Evidence: it counts twenty dogs, so it's less likely to be a fluke.** (answer) / Example: twenty dogs in one test is still just one story, like Rufus. / Expert: the company tested twenty dogs, so it knows its collar best. / Evidence: but it's weaker, because more dogs means more chances to go wrong.

Why: Twenty dogs is a count you could check. One dog might be a fluke, but twenty is far harder.

## 4. 3.7-b4-3 (Band 4)

> WELCOME TO THE RED ROCK WILDLIFE PARK. Nestled at the foot of Bluster Rock, our park is home to more than forty kinds of native animals, from tiny dunnarts to towering emus. Our keepers care for injured and orphaned animals and, where possible, return them to the wild. You'll meet every animal in Australia in one afternoon. The park is big enough to need its own little train, which stops at the aviary, the waterhole and the koala walk. Last year, 60,000 people visited the park. Every family on your street has been. Yours is the only one left. Who could resist meeting a bilby up close? Keeper talks are held every hour on the hour, and visitors can help feed the kangaroos at 2 pm. Entry costs $22 for adults and $11 for children. Is your child under four? Then entry is free. The park cafe sells sandwiches, ice creams and hot drinks, and picnic tables sit under the coolabah trees beside the waterhole. Wheelchairs and prams can travel on every path, and a night tour runs on Friday evenings in summer.

**Match each line from the brochure to its trick, or to 'no trick'.**

- You'll meet every animal in Australia in one afternoon. → **exaggeration**
- Every family on your street has been. Yours is the only one left. → **everyone's doing it**
- Who could resist meeting a bilby up close? → **rhetorical question**
- Is your child under four? Then entry is free. → **no trick**
- The park is big enough to need its own little train. → **no trick**
- Last year, 60,000 people visited the park. → **no trick**

Why: Every animal is blown up and your street is the crowd. The real question and true facts are no trick.

## 7. 3.2-b3-2 (Band 3)

> On Thursday, Year 6 held a debate about what the canteen should sell. Mo was the timekeeper, although he kept stopping the clock to tell jokes about sausages.

**Put each claim from the debate in the right box.**

- Last term, the fruit cups sold out every single day. The canteen should sell more fruit. → **backed-up claim: a reason or proof that supports it**
- Fruit is the best snack, because it is better than every other snack. → **bare claim: nothing supports it**
- Chips should stay on the menu, because I'd be really sad without them. → **bare claim: nothing supports it**
- Sushi rolls would sell well. Sixty students asked for them in the canteen survey. → **backed-up claim: a reason or proof that supports it**
- Salads would sell out every day, because lettuce is mostly water. → **bare claim: nothing supports it**
- Pies should stay, because the canteen makes more money from pies than anything else. → **backed-up claim: a reason or proof that supports it**
- The menu is perfect, because nobody could possibly want to change it. → **bare claim: nothing supports it**

Why: Backing must fit the claim and show why. A 'because' with a feeling or an off-topic fact holds nothing up.
