# What the teacher review found, and what we do about it

Sources: island-1-2.md, island-3-4.md, island-5.md, island-6-7.md and island-8-9-story.md in this folder.

## The two changes that matter most

1. **The I do states a rule. It doesn't teach the thinking.** All five reviews found this.
   - No Skill works an example aloud, names the common mistake, or checks understanding before practice.
   - Some taught rules are the misconception itself: 8.1 "the doer should come first", 8.2 "one whole thought", 8.3 "pick a time and stick to it", 7.3 "start with a bossy word", 6.5 "lift it out".
   - The 12-word cap on each beat made the lines clipped fragments.
   - **Fix:** redesign every I do as a worked example with the thinking said aloud, then a non-example that names the trap, then a quick check the student must pass. Use the format James picks from public/try/teach.html.
2. **Mastery can be earned without the Skill.** Students can pass by:
   - spotting a signal word (and/but means compound, "Dr" means expert);
   - reading the shape of the options;
   - tapping the last slot in fix tasks;
   - copying a Second look that names the answer;
   - using build tiles whose capital letters and full stops give the order away.

   Bands mostly add length, not thinking.
   - **Fix:** encode the shortcuts as content rules and rewrite against them. Then make each Band harder in the Skill itself: closer wrong options, and judging and explaining effects.

## Wave 1: now, no decision needed

- Fix every accuracy blocker and contested answer listed in the reviews.
- New rules, now in src/content/rules.ts:
  - a wrong-answer reason may not quote the right answer (91 found);
  - fix-task errors may not sit in the last slot more than half the time (16 Skills);
  - a We do check needs at least 3 options (10 found).
- Sort Second looks no longer name an item or its pile. When the answer is shown, every moved item shows its reason.
- Assumptions made, as a Victorian classroom would teach them:
  - the complication is the story's problem, as in orientation, complication, resolution;
  - "narrator" is the term, introduced as "the narrator, the one telling the story";
  - kid words are bridged once to the curriculum term, for example "joining word (conjunction)", "doer (subject)", "main clause (independent clause)".
